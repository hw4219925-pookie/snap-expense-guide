Understand the expense information used in SNAP estimates

SNAP calculations can consider more than household size and gross income. Keeping accurate expense records may improve the quality of an estimate and simplify an application.

Housing costs

Keep current records for rent or mortgage payments, property taxes, and homeowners insurance when applicable.

Utilities

Note which utilities your household is responsible for, such as heating, cooling, electricity, water, telephone, or trash service.

Dependent care

Document care expenses required so a household member can work, look for work, attend training, or pursue education.

Medical expenses

For elderly or disabled household members, retain records of qualifying out-of-pocket medical expenses that are not reimbursed.

Child support

Keep proof of legally obligated child-support payments made to someone outside the household.
